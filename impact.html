Global Impact

Advocacy that reaches home—and abroad.

Our advocacy pillar isn't limited to Mayo's hallways. We're channeling that same energy into building schools and orphanages overseas, starting with the Philippines.

Why this exists

The same advocacy, a bigger stage

Identifying a real problem, gathering evidence, finding the right partners, and following through until something changes — that's the advocacy pillar at Mayo. Global Impact is that exact process, aimed at a problem that reaches further than our campus: communities overseas that lack access to schools and safe housing for kids.

Overseas

Building schools & orphanages

Why the Philippines

A starting point, not a limit

We're starting with the Philippines because it's where our early connections and interest are strongest — but "Asian issues" don't stop at one country. As Mayo ASA grows, we want this initiative to grow with it, toward other underserved Asian communities that need the same kind of concrete support.
